# Prompt recipes

Phrasings that produced a usable effect on the first or second try. Copy one, adjust the
material or the mood word, keep the structure. The theme suffix is added automatically —
do not repeat "isolated", "dry" or "no music" here.

Each line is `name` · `duration` · `prompt`.

| --- | --- | --- |

## Ambience

Longer, and the least reliable family — the model wants events, not beds. Generate at
10 s and crossfade in the engine.

| Name | s | Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| forest_day | 10 | a quiet forest clearing, distant birdsong and light wind in leaves |
| cave_drip | 10 | a damp stone cave, occasional water drips with a long echo |
| camp_fire | 10 | a small campfire crackling steadily, close mic |
